# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial Earth observation: imagery, geospatial foundation models, and the tools and data products built on them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-07

---

- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Feb 2026) compares Earth [[Embeddings]] products, including Clay, Major TOM, Earth Index, Copernicus-Embed, Presto, Tessera and AlphaEarth. He argues that inconsistent formats, tile grids and CRS handling create integration debt, and calls for cloud-native standards, provenance and benchmarks. *Keywords: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, cloud-native geo, interoperability, AlphaEarth, standards*
