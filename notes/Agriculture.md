# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agricultural mapping, crop monitoring and farm-level data, often using [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-07

---

- [agribound (montimaj/agribound)](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): A [[Python]] toolkit that maps agricultural field boundaries from satellite imagery. It runs models such as Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, DINOv3 and Prithvi, or clusters [[Embeddings]] (Google Satellite Embedding, TESSERA), with optional SAM refinement. It works with Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS, NAIP and SPOT images. *Keywords: field boundary delineation, satellite imagery, foundation models, Google Earth Engine, SAM, crop mapping*
